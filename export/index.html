Export biostimulants and organic fertilizers from India

We supply importers, distributors and brand owners with biostimulants and organic inputs manufactured in Bhavnagar, Gujarat.

Gujarat Bio Organics exports humic acid, seaweed extract, amino acid, protein hydrolysate and biofertiliser products from Gujarat, India. FOB or CIF from Pipavav, Kandla, Dahej, Mundra and JNPT. No minimum order. Free samples.

Products for export

  • Spirulina and botanical extracts
  • Blended biostimulants and biofertilisers
