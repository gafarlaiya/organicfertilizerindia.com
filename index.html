Common questions

Who is Gujarat Bio Organics?

Gujarat Bio Organics Pvt. Ltd. is a biostimulant and organic fertilizer manufacturer based in Shihor, Bhavnagar, Gujarat, India. We make humic and fulvic acid, seaweed extract, protein hydrolysate, amino acid and biofertiliser products, and supply them under our Immunity brand, under your private label, or in bulk.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We quote against your requirement, including small trial orders.

Do you supply private label and OEM?

Yes. We formulate, manufacture and pack under your brand, label and pack design.

Which countries do you supply?

We supply GCC countries, Africa, the Russian Federation, Europe, North America and South America, and all of India. Export terms are FOB or CIF from Indian ports.

Do you give samples?

Yes. Samples are free. Freight or courier, customs duty and other charges are paid by the buyer.

How long does an order take?

Typically about one week.

Are your biostimulants approved?

The Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat has issued No Objection Certificates to us for 36 biostimulant grades (Schedule VI) and 11 biofertiliser types (Schedule III) under the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order 1985. Copies are on request.
