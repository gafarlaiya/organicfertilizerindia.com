Field solutions: start from the problem

Twenty years of R&D, product innovation and on-field work, turned into a plain list of what growers ask us and which grades we recommend.

Biostimulants and biofertilisers are tools for specific problems: soil structure, stress, flowering, recovery and nutrient uptake. Match the problem to the product family, run a small trial, and scale only what works on your farm.

Hard, tired soil that holds little water or nutrient

Repeated chemical use and low organic matter can leave soil compact, with poor structure and weak nutrient holding.

What it is used for
Humic and fulvic products are used to support soil structure, nutrient holding and root development.
Grades to ask for
Humic acid granular (basal), humate and fulvate liquids (drench or fertigation)

Heat, drought and salt stress

Crops stall in hot spells, dry spells or on saline patches.

What it is used for
Seaweed extracts are used to support crop tolerance to abiotic stress.
Grades to ask for
Ascophyllum nodosum, Kappaphycus alvarezii and Sargassum grades

Poor flowering and fruit set

Flowers drop or set is uneven in fruit, vegetable and cotton crops.

What it is used for
Seaweed and amino acid products are used around flowering to support set and development.
Grades to ask for
Seaweed + amino acid blends, seaweed + humic blends

Slow recovery after stress or transplanting

Crops look pale and slow after cold, pest damage, hail or transplanting.

What it is used for
Amino acids and protein hydrolysates are used to support recovery and early growth.
Grades to ask for
Amino acid and plant or animal source protein hydrolysate grades

Fertiliser that is not taken up well

Applied nutrients are lost or locked in the soil, so the crop uses less than the bag promises.

What it is used for
Biofertilisers help supply or release nitrogen, phosphorus, potash and zinc, and humic products help hold nutrients near roots.
Grades to ask for
Azotobacter, Azospirillum, PSB, KMB, ZSB, consortia, with humic acid

Weak root systems in the first weeks

Poor early rooting leads to uneven stand and slow canopy.

What it is used for
Humic and fulvic products and rhizosphere biofertilisers are used at sowing or transplanting to support root growth.
Grades to ask for
Humic liquids, Azospirillum, mycorrhizal biofertilisers

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Run a small trial first. These notes describe common uses and are not a guarantee of yield.

Questions from growers and buyers

Do your products guarantee a yield increase?

No. Results depend on crop, soil, weather and how the product is used. We describe what each product family is used for and recommend a small trial before wider use.

How do I choose a grade for my crop?

Share your crop, region, soil type and the problem you see. We recommend a grade, a dose and a timing, and can send a free sample for a trial.

Can distributors and brands use this information?

Yes. We can help you build crop-wise recommendations for your own brand and territory.
