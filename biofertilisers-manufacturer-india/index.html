Questions about biofertilisers

What are biofertilisers?

Biofertilisers are products that contain living microorganisms. Applied to seed, soil or plant, they help supply nutrients such as nitrogen and phosphorus, or make nutrients already in the soil easier for the crop to take up.

Which biofertiliser types do you make?

Azotobacter, Azospirillum, Acetobacter, Rhizobium, phosphate solubilising bacteria, potash mobilising bacteria, zinc solubilising bacteria, phosphate solubilising fungi, mycorrhizal biofertilisers, and carrier-based and liquid consortia.

Are biofertilisers approved?

The Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat has issued us a No Objection Certificate for 11 biofertiliser types under Schedule III of the Fertiliser Control Order.

Do you supply liquid and carrier-based forms?

Yes, liquid and carrier-based consortia are both on our list. Tell us the form and pack you need.
