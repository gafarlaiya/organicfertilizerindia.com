Private label and OEM biostimulant manufacturing in India

You have the market. We have the plant. We supply biostimulants and organic inputs under your brand name, label and packaging.

Gujarat Bio Organics offers private-label and contract manufacturing of biostimulants and biofertilisers from its own plant in Bhavnagar, Gujarat. No minimum order. Free sample. Typical dispatch in one week.

What we make for your brand

  • Humic and fulvic products: liquid, powder, granular
  • Seaweed extracts: Ascophyllum nodosum, Kappaphycus alvarezii, Sargassum
  • Protein hydrolysates and amino acid products, plant and animal source
  • Blends: humic + seaweed, seaweed + botanical, humic + amino acid + vitamins
  • Biofertilisers: Azotobacter, Azospirillum, PSB, KMB, Rhizobium, mycorrhizal, consortia
  • Custom formulations on request

Packing options

Liquids: 1000 L IBC, 220 L drum, and 50, 35, 25, 20, 10, 5 and 1 L, 500 mL and 250 mL packs.
Solids: 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10 and 20 kg bags or buckets, 25 and 50 kg bags, 600 kg and 1000 kg jumbo bags.

Why brands choose us

  • No minimum order: test a market before you scale
  • 100 KL liquid and 2,500 tonnes solid capacity every month
  • One manufacturer from formulation to packing
  • Third-party testing and sampling on request
  • Gujarat Directorate of Agriculture NOC for our biostimulant grades
  • Registered company since 2009 (CIN U24123GJ2009PTC058701)
