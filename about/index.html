About Gujarat Bio Organics

A Bhavnagar manufacturer of biostimulants and biofertilisers, built on R&D and field work.

Gujarat Bio Organics Pvt. Ltd. manufactures biostimulants and biofertilisers at Shihor, Bhavnagar, Gujarat. We combine 20 years of R&D, product innovation and hard on-field work with growers.

We make humic and fulvic acid, seaweed extract, protein hydrolysate, amino acid and biofertiliser products, and supply them three ways: under our Immunity brand, under your private label, or in bulk.

Our plant has capacity for 100 KL of liquid and 2,500 tonnes of solid products every month. We ship worldwide, with no minimum order and free samples.

How we work

  • Start from the farmer's problem, not the product
  • Trial first, scale what works
  • One manufacturer from formulation to packing
  • Plain terms: no MOQ, free samples, FOB or CIF
