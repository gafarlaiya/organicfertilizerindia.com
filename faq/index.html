Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our products, approvals, private label, distribution and export.

Company and supply

Who is Gujarat Bio Organics?

A manufacturer of organic fertilizers, biostimulants, biofertilisers and specialty fertilizers at Shihor, Bhavnagar, Gujarat, India, manufacturing since 2009.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We quote against your requirement, including small trial orders.

How long does dispatch take?

About one week from confirmed order for regular grades.

Do you give free samples?

Yes. Samples are free; freight or courier, customs duty and other charges are paid by the buyer.

What pack sizes do you offer?

Liquids: 250 mL, 500 mL, 1 L, 5 L, 10 L, 20 L, 25 L, 35 L, 50 L, 220 L drums and 1000 L IBC. Solids: 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10 and 20 kg bags or buckets, 25 and 50 kg bags, 600 kg and 1000 kg jumbo bags.

Products

What do you manufacture?

Humic and fulvic acids, seaweed extracts, protein hydrolysates and amino acids, botanical and blended biostimulants (36 grades), 11 biofertiliser types, and micronutrient, water-soluble, slow-release and custom fertilizers made to order.

Do you make zyme granules?

Yes, from Schedule VI listed granular grades: humic acid granules, Sargassum seaweed granules and humic plus seaweed granules.

Do you make crop-specific products?

Yes. Our Immunity range has dedicated products for sugarcane, potato, mango, apple and banana, and programmes for cotton, citrus and coconut.

Do you publish prices?

Price depends on the grade and concentration, the form (liquid, powder or granule), the pack size, the order quantity and, for export, the Incoterm and destination port. We do not publish a price list because bulk, private-label and retail packs are priced differently. Send the grade, quantity and destination and we reply with a quote, usually within one working day.

Compliance

Are your biostimulants approved?

Our No Objection Certificate from the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat covers 36 Schedule VI biostimulant grades and 11 Schedule III biofertiliser types. Copies are on request.

Can you provide test reports?

Yes. Specification sheets and third-party test reports are available, and third-party sampling can be arranged.

What changed for biostimulants in 2025?

Provisional registrations ended in June 2025, and only biostimulants specified in Schedule VI can now be sold in India.

Private label, distribution and export

Can you make products under my brand?

Yes, with your label and pack design and no minimum order.

How do distributor margins work?

We give you bulk prices and you set your own selling margin.

Which countries do you export to?

GCC countries, Africa, the Russian Federation, Europe, North America and South America, FOB or CIF from Indian ports.
