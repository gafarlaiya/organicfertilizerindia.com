Seaweed extract uses in agriculture: benefits and how to apply

What seaweed extract does for crops, liquid vs granules, when to apply, and what to check when buying.

Seaweed extract is used in agriculture to help crops cope with heat, drought and salinity, to support flowering and fruit set, and to support root and shoot growth and recovery after stress. Liquid extracts are applied as foliar sprays or through drip; seaweed granules are broadcast with basal fertilizer.

What growers use it for

  • Getting through heat waves, dry spells and saline patches
  • Flowering, fruit set and fruit development
  • Root growth after sowing and transplanting
  • Recovery after hail, frost or pest damage
  • Quality in fruit and vegetable crops

Liquid, powder or granules

Liquid extracts are the most common and suit foliar spray and drip. Powders are convenient to store and ship and dissolve for spraying. Granules carry seaweed into the soil with basal fertilizer, which suits farmers who broadcast rather than spray.

When to apply

Seaweed works best before or at the start of stress, and at key stages: establishment, the start of flowering, fruit set and early fruit development. Spray in the cooler part of the day.

What to check when buying

  • The seaweed species and the extract percentage
  • That the grade is specified in Schedule VI if sold in India
  • A specification sheet and test report
  • The dose per acre for the concentration you are paying for

Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of seaweed extract for plants?

It supports tolerance to heat, drought and salinity, flowering and fruit set, root and shoot growth, and recovery after stress.

How is seaweed extract made?

Seaweed is processed to break open the cells and release soluble compounds, which are concentrated to the specified extract content.

Can seaweed extract be mixed with humic acid?

Yes. They are often applied together, and we make ready humic plus seaweed blends.

Is seaweed extract organic?

It is derived from natural seaweed. Whether a product is permitted in certified organic farming depends on the certifying standard and the processing used.

What are seaweed granules used for?

Broadcasting with basal fertilizer or at top dressing to bring seaweed into the root zone.
