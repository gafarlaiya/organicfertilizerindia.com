Potassium humate vs humic acid: what is the difference?

Potassium humate is soluble humic acid. Here is how the two differ, what grades like 49% and 98% mean, and which to use.

Potassium humate is humic acid that has been neutralised with potassium, which makes it soluble in water. Humic acid itself is insoluble in acid and is usually applied to soil as granules or powder. Choose potassium humate for drip, drench, foliar and liquid formulation, and humic acid granules for basal application.

What 49% and 98% mean

The percentage on a label describes content, but different labels measure different things: humic acid content, total humic and fulvic content, or solubility. That is why a '98%' product and a '49%' product cannot be compared on the number alone. In India, sell and buy the grade exactly as specified in Schedule VI. Our listed grade is potassium humate 49% powder.

How potassium humate is used

  • Dissolved for drip and fertigation
  • Soil drench around the root zone
  • Mixed into liquid fertilizers and biostimulants by brand owners

Frequently asked questions

Is potassium humate the same as humic acid?

It is humic acid in its soluble potassium salt form.

What is potassium humate 98?

It usually means high-solubility potassium humate flakes or powder sold on a total-content basis. Compare specifications, not just the number.

What are the uses of potassium humate 49 powder?

Drip, drench and foliar programmes, and as a soluble humic base for liquid formulations.

Is potassium humate a fertilizer?

In India it is regulated as a biostimulant grade under Schedule VI, not as a potash fertilizer.
