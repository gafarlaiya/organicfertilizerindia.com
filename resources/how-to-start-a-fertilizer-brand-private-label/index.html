How to start your own fertilizer brand with private label

A step-by-step guide to launching an organic fertilizer or biostimulant brand without building a factory: products, compliance, packaging and costs.

You can start a fertilizer or biostimulant brand without a factory by working with a private-label manufacturer: choose products and grades, get the authorisations needed to sell in your market, approve samples and label design, and have the manufacturer produce and pack under your brand. In India, biostimulants must be Schedule VI listed grades.

By the Gujarat Bio Organics R&D team · Updated October 2026

  1. Step by step

Step by step

  1. Choose your market and crops. Who will buy, where, and what problem you solve.
  2. Pick products. Start with three to five proven grades, for example humic, seaweed and amino acid.
  3. Check compliance. In India, selling fertilizers and biostimulants needs state authorisation under the FCO, and biostimulants must be listed grades. Abroad, check your country's registration rules.
  4. Get samples and test. Run field trials and lab checks.
  5. Design labels and packs. Include the grade, batch, dates and manufacturer details your rules require.
  6. Place the first order. With no minimum order, start small.
  7. Build demand. Field demonstrations, farmer meetings and dealer networks.

What it costs

The main costs are product, packaging and label printing, registration or authorisation fees, and marketing. Starting with existing listed grades avoids development cost and speeds up launch.

Choosing a manufacturer

  • Holds approvals for the grades you want
  • Accepts small first orders
  • Can pack in the sizes your market buys
  • Gives specification sheets and test reports
  • Has R&D to build custom products later

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a licence to sell fertilizer under my brand in India?

Yes. Selling fertilizers and biostimulants needs authorisation from the state agriculture department under the Fertiliser Control Order. Check current requirements with your state department.

What is the minimum order for private label?

With us there is no fixed minimum order quantity.

How long does a private-label launch take?

Once samples, label and pack are approved, production is about one week.

What is white label fertilizer?

Another term for private label: a manufacturer's product sold under your brand.
