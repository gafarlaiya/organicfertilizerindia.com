About Humic acid + seaweed extract

What it is

Blended grades combine two or more biostimulant materials, for example humic acid with seaweed extract, so one application covers more than one job. Each blend on this site is a listed grade under our Gujarat NOC, so it can be sold as supplied or packed under your brand.

Typical uses

  • One-pass programmes that cut the number of applications
  • Combining soil action (humic) with stress support (seaweed)
  • Ready-to-sell crop programmes for distributors and private-label brands

How to use it

  • Basal or broadcast (granules and powders)
  • Drip or fertigation (liquids)
  • Foliar spray (liquids)

The dose depends on crop, stage and method. It is printed on the label, and we send a crop-wise dose chart with every quote.

Mixing

Blends are designed to be stable as supplied. Jar-test before tank-mixing with other products.

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Follow the label, run a small trial first and scale what works on your farm. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of yield.

Questions about this grade

What is Humic acid + seaweed extract used for?

Humic acid and seaweed extract as a soluble powder, convenient to store and ship. It is commonly used for: one-pass programmes that cut the number of applications; combining soil action (humic) with stress support (seaweed); ready-to-sell crop programmes for distributors and private-label brands.

How is Humic acid + seaweed extract applied?

Common methods are basal or broadcast (granules and powders), drip or fertigation (liquids), foliar spray (liquids). The dose is printed on the label, and we send a crop-wise dose chart with every quote.

Is Humic acid + seaweed extract an approved grade?

Yes. Humic acid + seaweed extract (powder) is a grade specified in Schedule VI of the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order 1985, and it is covered by the No Objection Certificate issued to Gujarat Bio Organics by the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat.

What is the price of Humic acid + seaweed extract?

Price depends on the grade and concentration, the form (liquid, powder or granule), the pack size, the order quantity and, for export, the Incoterm and destination port. Can I buy Humic acid + seaweed extract under my own brand?

Yes. We manufacture and pack it under your private label with no minimum order quantity, in solid packs from 1 kg buckets to 1000 kg jumbo bags.

Do you export Humic acid + seaweed extract?
