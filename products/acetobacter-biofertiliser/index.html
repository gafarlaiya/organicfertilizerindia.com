About Acetobacter

How it works

An endophytic nitrogen-fixing bacterium (Gluconacetobacter diazotrophicus) best known for living inside sugarcane tissue, where it supplies nitrogen from within the plant.

Biofertilisers work best in soil with organic matter, moisture and a near-neutral pH. They add to, rather than replace, a balanced fertilizer programme, and they take time to establish, so apply them early in the crop.

Application methods

  • Seed treatment before sowing
  • Seedling root dip before transplanting
  • Soil application mixed with compost or farmyard manure
  • Drip application (liquid forms)

Handling

  • Store cool and shaded, away from sunlight
  • Keep apart from chemical fungicides and bactericides
  • Use within the printed shelf life

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Follow the label, run a small trial first and scale what works on your farm. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of yield.

Questions about this biofertiliser

What is Acetobacter?

An endophytic nitrogen-fixing bacterium (Gluconacetobacter diazotrophicus) best known for living inside sugarcane tissue, where it supplies nitrogen from within the plant.

Which crops is Acetobacter used for?

Commonly: Sugarcane, Sugar beet and sweet sorghum.

How is Acetobacter applied?

Common methods are seed treatment, seedling root dip, soil application mixed with compost or farmyard manure, and drip application for liquid forms. Follow the label for dose and method.

Can Acetobacter be mixed with chemical fertilizers or fungicides?

Do not mix biofertilisers directly with chemical fungicides, bactericides or concentrated fertilizers, because they can kill the organisms. Apply them separately, a few days apart, or follow the label.

How should Acetobacter be stored?

Store in a cool, shaded place away from direct sunlight and heat, and use within the shelf life printed on the pack.

Is Acetobacter an approved product?

Yes. Acetobacter is among the 11 biofertiliser types specified in Schedule III of the Fertiliser Control Order that are covered by the No Objection Certificate issued to Gujarat Bio Organics by the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat.
