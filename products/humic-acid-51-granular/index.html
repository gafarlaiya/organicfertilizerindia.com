About Humic acid 51%

What it is

Humic substances are the stable, dark fraction of well-decomposed organic matter. Commercial humic acid is usually extracted from humified sources such as leonardite or lignite by an alkaline process, and potassium humate is its water-soluble potassium salt. Fulvic acids are the smaller, lighter fraction that stays dissolved across a wide pH range, which is why fulvic-rich grades suit foliar and drip use.

Typical uses

  • Soil conditioning in compact, low organic-matter or over-cropped soils
  • Holding nutrients near the root zone so less is lost from the profile
  • Complexing micronutrients and improving their availability
  • Supporting early root development at sowing or transplanting
  • Getting more value from the fertiliser already being applied

How to use it

  • Basal application mixed with fertiliser (granules and powders)
  • Drip or fertigation (liquids and soluble powders)
  • Soil drench around the root zone
  • Foliar spray (fulvic and fully soluble grades)

The dose depends on crop, stage and method. It is printed on the label, and we send a crop-wise dose chart with every quote.

Mixing

Humic grades mix with most fertilisers. Avoid tank-mixing with strongly acidic products or high-calcium solutions without a jar test, because humic acid can precipitate at low pH.

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Follow the label, run a small trial first and scale what works on your farm. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of yield.

Questions about this grade

What is Humic acid 51% used for?

A high-content humic granule for basal application, broadcast or mixed with fertiliser at sowing and top dressing. It is commonly used for: soil conditioning in compact, low organic-matter or over-cropped soils; holding nutrients near the root zone so less is lost from the profile; complexing micronutrients and improving their availability.

Yes. Humic acid 51% (granular) is a grade specified in Schedule VI of the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order 1985, and it is covered by the No Objection Certificate issued to Gujarat Bio Organics by the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat.
