About Protein hydrolysate, animal source 36.04%

What it is

Protein hydrolysates are mixtures of free amino acids and short peptides made by breaking down proteins with enzymes, acid or alkali. Plant-source grades start from vegetable proteins; animal-source grades start from animal proteins such as collagen, which gives a profile richer in glycine and proline. Amino acid grades are specified by their free amino acid content.

Typical uses

  • Supporting recovery after cold, heat, transplanting or pest damage
  • Early growth and canopy development
  • Flowering and fruit set programmes
  • Complexing micronutrients in foliar formulations

How to use it

  • Foliar spray
  • Drip or fertigation
  • Formulation base for brand owners

The dose depends on crop, stage and method. It is printed on the label, and we send a crop-wise dose chart with every quote.

Mixing

Amino acid products mix with most foliar nutrients and seaweed extracts. Jar-test before mixing with copper or strongly alkaline products.

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Follow the label, run a small trial first and scale what works on your farm. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of yield.
