Cotton nutrition and biostimulant programme

How cotton growers in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan use humic acid, seaweed, amino acids and biofertilisers, and how our Immunity cotton programme came about.

Cotton needs a good start, steady growth into squaring, strong retention of squares, flowers and bolls, and boll filling. Growers combine balanced NPK with humic acid and biofertilisers at sowing, boron and other micronutrients where deficient, and seaweed extract and amino acids at squaring and flowering to support retention under stress.

Cotton questions

What is the best fertilizer for cotton crop?

Cotton needs balanced NPK by soil test, with attention to boron and zinc. Humic acid at sowing and seaweed extract at squaring and flowering are common additions.

Which micronutrient is important for cotton?

Boron is especially important for flowering and boll retention, and zinc is often deficient. Apply on a soil or leaf test basis.

What foliar spray is used in cotton?

Growers commonly spray boron and micronutrients, seaweed extract and amino acids at squaring and flowering, following a crop schedule.

How can I reduce flower drop in cotton?

Flower and square shedding is caused by water stress, heat, nutrient gaps such as boron, and pests. Fix water and nutrition first; seaweed and amino acid sprays are commonly used to support retention.
