Apple nutrition and biostimulant programme

How apple growers in Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir and Uttarakhand use humic acid, seaweed, amino acids and biofertilisers, and how our Immunity Apple Special came about.

Apple orchards need healthy roots and soil, good spring flowering and set, steady fruit development and colour, and post-harvest recovery. Growers combine balanced nutrition with humic acid and mycorrhizae for the root zone, calcium and boron where needed, seaweed extract around flowering and fruit development, and amino acids after frost or hail.

Apple questions

What is the best fertilizer for apple trees?

Apple needs balanced nutrition on a soil and leaf test basis, with attention to calcium and boron for fruit quality. Humic acid, mycorrhizae and seaweed extract are commonly added to support roots, flowering and fruit development.

Which fertilizer improves apple fruit size and colour?

Fruit size and colour depend on crop load, sunlight, potash and calcium nutrition and tree health. Seaweed extract is commonly sprayed during fruit development as part of a quality programme.

What should apple growers apply after hail?

After hail, growers commonly spray amino acids or seaweed extract to support recovery, alongside the crop-protection measures their local advisers recommend.

Why are apple trees dying in my orchard?

Tree decline has many causes, including root diseases, waterlogging, poor soil health, nutrient imbalance and pests. Get a field diagnosis; root-zone health programmes are often part of the recovery plan.
