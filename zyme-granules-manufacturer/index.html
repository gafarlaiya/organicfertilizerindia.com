What zyme granules are, and what to check

A granule makes a biostimulant easy to apply: the farmer broadcasts it with basal fertilizer or at earthing up, without spraying. In the Indian market these products are widely sold as zyme, seaweed or soil granules.

Why the grade on the bag matters now

Biostimulants in India came under Schedule VI of the Fertiliser Control Order in 2021, with a provisional registration period. Industry reports say provisional registrations ended on 17 June 2025, after which only biostimulants specified in Schedule VI can be sold. Granules made from listed grades protect distributors and brands from stock that can no longer be sold. See our Schedule VI guide.

What are zyme granules made of?

Composition varies by maker. Most carry seaweed extract, humic acid, protein hydrolysate or a mix on a granular carrier. Ours carry the listed grade printed on the bag.

Questions buyers ask

What are zyme granules?

Broadcast granules that carry biostimulants such as seaweed extract, humic acid or protein hydrolysate. 'Zyme' is a trade term in India, not a regulated category.

What is the composition of zyme granules?

It varies by manufacturer. Ours are made from Schedule VI listed grades such as humic acid 51% granular, Sargassum tenerrimum 2% granular and humic acid plus seaweed extract granules.

What are the uses of zyme granules?

They are broadcast with basal fertilizer or at earthing up to support root growth and soil conditioning through the season.

What is the price of zyme granules per kg or per bucket?

Price depends on the grade and concentration, the form (liquid, powder or granule), the pack size, the order quantity and, for export, the Incoterm and destination port. We do not publish a price list because bulk, private-label and retail packs are priced differently. Send the grade, quantity and destination and we reply with a quote, usually within one working day.

What pack sizes do you offer?

1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10 and 20 kg bags or buckets, 25 and 50 kg bags, and 600 kg and 1000 kg jumbo bags.

Are zyme granules legal to sell after June 2025?

Biostimulants must be specified in Schedule VI of the Fertiliser Control Order to be sold in India. Check that the grade on the bag is a listed grade. Ours are.

Seaweed granules or humic granules: which is better?

Humic granules mainly condition the soil and support roots; seaweed granules add stress support. Our humic plus seaweed granules combine both.
