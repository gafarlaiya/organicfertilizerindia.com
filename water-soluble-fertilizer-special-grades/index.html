Water-soluble fertilizers and special grade fertilizers

Fully soluble NPK special grades for drip and foliar feeding, and special fortified grades that combine nutrients with humic, seaweed or amino acids, made to order.

Water-soluble fertilizers (WSF) are 100% soluble NPK grades used in drip irrigation and foliar sprays to feed a crop at the exact stage it needs each nutrient. Gujarat Bio Organics makes special grade and fortified fertilizers to order for brands, distributors and importers, including blends with humic acid, seaweed extract and amino acids.

How special grades are chosen

Each water-soluble grade is named by its N:P:K ratio, and each ratio suits a different crop stage. Growers commonly use balanced grades such as 19:19:19 in vegetative growth, phosphorus-rich grades such as 12:61:0 at establishment, and potash-rich grades such as 13:0:45 or 0:0:50 at fruit development. Calcium and magnesium grades and micronutrient-fortified grades complete the range.

Special grades become more useful when they carry a biostimulant. Humic acid and seaweed extract are commonly combined with soluble NPK so one application feeds the crop and supports root activity or stress tolerance.

Questions buyers ask

What is water-soluble fertilizer?

A fertilizer that dissolves completely in water, so it can be applied through drip irrigation or as a foliar spray and is taken up quickly.

What are the common water-soluble fertilizer grades?

Common grades include 19:19:19, 20:20:20, 12:61:0, 13:40:13, 13:0:45, 0:52:34, 0:0:50, calcium nitrate and magnesium sulphate. Each suits a different crop stage.

What is 19:19:19 fertilizer used for?

It is a balanced NPK grade used mainly in vegetative growth through drip or foliar spray.

When should 13:0:45 and 0:52:34 be used?

Potash-rich 13:0:45 is commonly used at fruit development; 0:52:34 supplies phosphorus and potash, commonly at flowering. Follow a crop-wise schedule.

What are special grade fertilizers?

Grades formulated for a particular crop, stage or need, often fortified with micronutrients or biostimulants such as humic acid, seaweed or amino acids.
