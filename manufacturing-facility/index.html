Manufacturing facility, Shihor, Bhavnagar

Liquid, powder, granule and biofertiliser production under one roof in GIDC Shihor, Gujarat, with packing from 250 mL bottles to 1000 L IBCs and jumbo bags.

Gujarat Bio Organics manufactures at its own plant at Plot No. 321, GIDC 2, Ghanghli Road, Shihor, Bhavnagar, Gujarat 364240. The plant has capacity for 100 KL of liquid and 2,500 tonnes of solid products each month, with in-house packing for retail, private-label and bulk orders.

What happens in the plant

Liquid formulation and filling

Seaweed, humic, fulvic, amino acid and blended liquids, filled from 250 mL bottles to 220 L drums and 1000 L IBCs.

Powder blending and packing

Soluble humate, humic, amino and blended powders, packed in bags and buckets.

Granulation

Humic, seaweed and protein granules for broadcast, from 1 kg buckets to 1000 kg jumbo bags.

Biofertiliser production

Liquid and carrier-based biofertilisers and consortia.

Private-label packing

Your label, your pack design, your carton, packed on our lines.

Quality control

Batch checks in-house, with third-party sampling and testing available on request.

Facility questions

Where is your factory?

Our plant is at Plot No. 321, GIDC 2, Ghanghli Road, Shihor, Bhavnagar, Gujarat 364240, India.

What is your production capacity?

100 KL of liquid products and 2,500 tonnes of granular and solid products per month.

What pack sizes can you fill?

Liquids: 250 mL, 500 mL, 1 L, 5 L, 10 L, 20 L, 25 L, 35 L, 50 L, 220 L drums and 1000 L IBC. Solids: 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10 and 20 kg bags or buckets, 25 and 50 kg bags, 600 kg and 1000 kg jumbo bags.

How fast can you dispatch?

About one week from confirmed order, for regular grades.

Can I visit the plant or send an inspector?

Yes. Plant visits and third-party sampling and testing can be arranged in advance.
