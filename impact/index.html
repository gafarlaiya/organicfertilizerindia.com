Two decades on real farms

We have spent 20 years solving growers' burning problems in the field, across crops and regions. Now the formula is open to the world.

Gujarat Bio Organics has worked with growers on their own farms for about 20 years, across sugarcane, potato, mango, apple, banana, citrus, coconut and cotton in many regions of India. What we learned became our Immunity range, our R&D and the grades we make for brands and importers. We now offer that proven formula to partners worldwide.

Burning problems we have worked on

Each programme started with a problem growers brought to us, often one with no chemical answer.

Apple

Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir

Growers faced plant mortality, low production, poor fruit quality and rising input costs, with no chemical cure on offer. Thousands of apple growers have since used our programme.

Potato

Punjab and Haryana

Declining yields, heavy fertiliser requirements, fewer and smaller tubers and weak quality. Thousands of potato growers have used our programme.

Sugarcane

South Gujarat

Land under cane for about five decades, tired soil, rising chemical use and no break-even. Growers who adopted the programme told us it brought them back to break-even.

Mango

Across India

Tree mortality, poor growth, alternate bearing, fruit drop, small fruit and poor quality. Several thousand mango growers have used our programme.

Banana

Central Gujarat

Sigatoka and Fusarium disease pressure, plant losses and weak bunches. Growers reported near-zero mortality and heavy, export-quality bunches.

Citrus and coconut

Multiple regions

Thousands of citrus and coconut growers have used our products. Hear a coconut grower in his own words in our videos.

Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice. Follow the label, run a small trial first and scale what works on your farm. Nothing on this page is a guarantee of yield.

Open to the world

Our mission is to make hazardous-chemical-free, sustainable food cultivation easier for everyone.

Chemical-free farming that works for the grower

Our vision is a world where food is grown without hazardous chemicals, and where growing that way is easier, not harder. That means products a grower can trust to do real work on a real farm, and the support to use them well.

For 20 years we tested ours in the field before we scaled them. Now we are opening them to the world so that more growers can farm sustainably, for generations to come.

Questions

How long has Gujarat Bio Organics been working with growers?

For about two decades. Our R&D team has worked with growers on their own farms across crops and regions, and every crop programme we offer comes from that work.

Which crops have you worked on?

Sugarcane, potato, mango, apple, banana, citrus, coconut and cotton are the crops we have the most field experience with. See the crop pages for the programmes.

Can I see growers talking about their results?

Yes. Growers share their own experience in videos on our YouTube channel and Facebook page. Those are individual experiences. Results vary with crop, soil, weather and practice.

What does it mean that the formula is open to the world?

The products and know-how built over 20 years are available to importers, brand owners, distributors and growers in any country, with no minimum order, free samples and your own label if you want it.

Do your products replace all chemical inputs?

No claim like that is made. Our aim is to help growers cut dependence on hazardous chemical inputs step by step, with trials first. Follow the label and run a small trial on your own farm.
