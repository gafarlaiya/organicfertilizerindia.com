Questions about biostimulants

What is a biostimulant under Indian law?

In India, biostimulants are regulated under Schedule VI of the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order 1985. Each product needs an approved grade. Our No Objection Certificate from the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat covers 36 grades.

What is the difference between Ascophyllum, Kappaphycus and Sargassum extracts?

They are different seaweeds. Ascophyllum nodosum is a cold-water brown alga, Kappaphycus alvarezii is a red alga farmed in warm waters, and Sargassum is a tropical brown alga. Each gives a different composition, so the right grade depends on your crop and use.
